# Front pronunciation aid

English (US) only; displayed below playback controls on the front and front editor/preview. Never displayed on the back. No additional stored card fields or database changes.

CMU dictionary 3.0.0 is loaded lazily as a cached static chunk (~961 kB gzip). No paid API or card text sent to a third party. ARPABET is converted to approximate English respelling with uppercase primary stress. This is not IPA or a transcription of the selected device voice. US-only: British English is deliberately excluded to avoid accent mismatch.

Conservative coverage: up to 12 dictionary words, no numbers/unsupported symbols, unknown words or alternate CMU pronunciations cause the complete hint to be omitted. Connected speech is not modeled. No extra warning is displayed. An accessible label/tooltip identifies the approximation as American English. Existing speech/recorded audio, language behavior and study state are unchanged.

Example: million → MIL-yuhn. Dictionary licenses: public/pronunciation-licenses.txt.
